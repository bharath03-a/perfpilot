The agent that finds the commit that slowed your deployment — and proves it.

PerfPilot keeps a vLLM deployment of a Meta open model fast. PerfBisect finds the first slow commit; TuneSmith tries to win speed back with serve flags. The brain proposes — benchmarks dispose. Every claim carries a statistical interval, or it doesn't ship.

Two planes

A free local control plane decides and proves; a metered Modal A100 plane only measures. The Baseten brain only advises.

$0 · local

Control plane

Bisect driver, Welch-interval stats, TuneSmith, HTML reports. Every agent proposal is schema-validated with a deterministic fallback — runs complete with no key.

$2.10/h · Modal A100-40GB

Measurement plane

One warm container serves one commit's wheel at a time; pinned GPU, same-UUID enforced, failed requests invalidate the run. Trials are the only spend.
